Aquarium Support

Aquarium turns your Mac desktop into a living aquarium or terrarium while keeping desktop icons, Finder, and normal clicks usable.

Quick help

Use the fish icon in the menu bar to open Settings, pause, feed animals, switch worlds, or quit. Closing Settings leaves the habitat running. Each world remembers its own creatures and decorations.

Requirements

macOS 13 or later and a Metal-capable Mac. Apple silicon and Intel are supported.
