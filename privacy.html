Aquarium Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

Aquarium does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or usage data.

Data stored on your Mac

Your habitat choices, creature quantities, decorations, and visual preferences are stored locally in macOS preferences. Aquarium does not provide these settings to the developer or to third parties.

Network access and third parties

Aquarium does not require an account or internet connection. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking technology, or third-party software development kits.

Desktop interaction

Aquarium reads the current pointer position to let nearby animals react. This information is processed on your Mac and is not recorded or transmitted. Aquarium does not read, move, or upload desktop files.

Changes

If a future version changes these practices, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to aquariumdesktop@icloud.com.
